# Phase 2 — Content Briefs

**Status:** Proposed for owner review  
**Purpose:** Translate the approved information architecture into page-level content requirements before implementation.

## Messaging foundation

The working positioning is:

> OCSCO integrates strategy, design, and technology to build digital infrastructure for ambitious brands.

This is a working direction, not final published copy. Final claims, metrics, client references, and proof points require owner approval and evidence.

Voice requirements from the existing brand reference:

- Confident, direct, intelligent, composed, and warm.
- Lead with outcomes and clarity, not generic process language.
- Prefer short declarative sentences and active voice.
- Avoid hype, vague superlatives, buzzwords, exclamation marks, and unverified promises.
- Frame OCSCO as a partner and architect, not a commodity vendor.

## Homepage brief

**Job:** Establish credibility quickly, explain OCSCO's integrated capability, and move qualified visitors toward a conversation.

**Required content:**

- One outcome-led hero statement.
- One short supporting explanation.
- Primary CTA: Start a Conversation.
- Secondary CTA: See How We Work or Explore the Work.
- Credibility signal using only approved client names, logos, or evidence.
- Four or five capability summaries linked to service pages.
- Short philosophy or differentiation section.
- One featured case study with approved evidence.
- Closing conversion section.

**Content questions:**

- What is the single most important outcome OCSCO wants to be known for?
- Which service or capability should lead the first viewport?
- Which proof point is strong enough to appear before a visitor scrolls deeply?

## Services brief

**Job:** Present capabilities as an integrated system rather than a flat list.

**Required content:**

- A positioning statement about how the capabilities work together.
- Five service cards or equivalent capability summaries.
- For each service: who it is for, the business problem, the outcome, and the engagement boundary.
- A simple process explanation.
- Links to relevant approved case studies.
- A low-pressure CTA to discuss a project.

**Service detail brief:**

Each detail page should answer:

1. What is this capability?
2. Who needs it?
3. What changes when OCSCO does it well?
4. What does the engagement include?
5. What evidence supports the claim?
6. What is the next conversation?

## Work brief

**Job:** Prove quality and strategic depth with specific, approved examples.

**Required content:**

- A project index with clear service/category filters only if there are enough projects to justify them.
- At least one detailed case study before launch claims are made.
- Project context, challenge, approach, deliverables, and outcomes.
- Named client information, imagery, metrics, and testimonials only with permission.

## About brief

**Job:** Build trust by explaining how OCSCO thinks and works.

**Required content:**

- A clear belief or philosophy statement.
- The integrated strategy/design/technology model.
- Working principles or values with specific explanations.
- Accurate team or leadership information when approved.
- A bridge to Services or Contact.

## Contact brief

**Job:** Make a qualified inquiry feel easy, clear, and low pressure.

**Required content:**

- An inviting headline.
- Short explanation of what happens after contact.
- Approved contact method or form fields.
- Response expectation and responsible owner.
- Alternative contact method if approved.

## Launch content inventory

The following inputs are required before public implementation:

- Approved positioning statement and homepage headline.
- Final service names and descriptions.
- At least one approved case study with evidence.
- Approved client logos, testimonials, and photography, if used.
- Team names, roles, and imagery, if the About page includes the team.
- Contact email or form destination and response workflow.
- Approved social sharing image and metadata.
