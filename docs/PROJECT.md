# Project 0 — Product Vision

OCSCO is replacing its existing WordPress website with a custom platform built for long-term ownership and extension. Project Crimson is the repository and working name for this platform.

The finished platform is intended to serve two connected purposes:

- A polished public OCSCO website.
- An internal platform that supports a custom CMS and a custom CRM.

The future CMS will give OCSCO control over structured website content and publishing workflows. The future CRM will support business operations and relationship management. They are planned as integrated parts of the same OCSCO platform, not unrelated products.

OCSCO wants the platform to demonstrate its ability to deliver branding, website design and development, custom CMS systems, CRM and business tools, and custom web applications. Project 0 is intended to become OCSCO's first flagship case study.

The current focus is infrastructure and engineering foundation. Visual design, public marketing content, CMS functionality, CRM functionality, and production integrations are outside Phase 0.
