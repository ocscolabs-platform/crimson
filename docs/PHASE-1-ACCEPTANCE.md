# Phase 1 — Acceptance Criteria

Phase 1 is complete when the owner can approve the information architecture as a stable input to content planning and visual design.

## Required outcomes

- [ ] The primary audiences are confirmed and prioritized.
- [ ] The public sitemap is approved, including required and deferred routes.
- [ ] The primary navigation and primary conversion action are approved.
- [ ] Every required route has a clear purpose, audience, and next action.
- [ ] The initial service taxonomy is approved or explicitly reduced.
- [ ] The case study requirements and publication permissions are understood.
- [ ] The initial structured content types and relationships are approved.
- [ ] Content ownership and review expectations are identified for launch content.
- [ ] SEO and sharing fields are included in the content model for all indexable page types.
- [ ] Draft, published, and deferred content states are understood.

## Quality and safety constraints

- No public website routes beyond the current foundation shell are implemented in Phase 1.
- No CMS, CRM, authentication, database, or storage functionality is implemented in Phase 1.
- No production credentials, client data, or unpublished case-study information is added to the repository.
- The proposed structure remains simple enough to operate without a CMS during initial content review.
- Any change to the planned platform boundaries is recorded in `docs/DECISIONS.md`.

## Phase exit review

The owner should review:

1. [`INFORMATION-ARCHITECTURE.md`](./INFORMATION-ARCHITECTURE.md)
2. [`CONTENT-MODEL.md`](./CONTENT-MODEL.md)
3. This acceptance checklist

When the checklist is approved, the next phase can define content briefs, visual design direction, and the implementation boundary for the first public routes.
